# GameBEX name and logo

The code is under AGPL-3.0-or-later (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The GameBEX name and logo
aren't. They tell people a build comes from this project, and they belong to
Jeff Nash.

## Fine without asking

- Saying your project is "based on GameBEX" or "a fork of GameBEX".
- Sharing unmodified official builds or source, free of charge, under the
  GameBEX name.
- Writing about, reviewing or making videos of GameBEX.

## Needs its own name

- A changed build or fork that you distribute. Give it its own name and icon,
  and keep the AGPL notices and the credit to GameBEX.
- Anything sold, bundled with paid hardware or listed in an app store under the
  GameBEX name or logo.
- Anything that suggests this project endorses you.

To ask about anything else, open an issue.
