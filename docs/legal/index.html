Legal

What GameBEX ships, what it does not, and what stays your responsibility.

Source

This page is built from docs/LEGAL.md in the GameBEX repository.

GameBEX is a free, open-source frontend and library manager for playing legally owned video games on Android. This document states what the project is, what it is not, and what responsibilities stay with you, the user. It is not legal advice; when in doubt, consult a lawyer in your jurisdiction.

1. No copyrighted content ships with this project

Section titled “1. No copyrighted content ships with this project”

The repository and its release APKs contain no games, ROMs, disc images, BIOS/firmware files, keys, artwork, or download catalogs. Anything playable or firmware-shaped on your device got there because you put it there: files you copied, sources you imported, or fetches you explicitly approved.

2. Only play what you legally own

Section titled “2. Only play what you legally own”
  • Only add games you own and, where your jurisdiction requires it, dumped from media you physically possess. Downloading a ROM for a game you do not own is copyright infringement in most places — including “I own it on another platform” in many of them.
  • “Abandonware”, “out of print”, and “the publisher is defunct” are not legal categories. Assume everything is still copyrighted.
  • Homebrew, demoscene, and freely licensed games are the worry-free way to try the app.

3. BIOS and firmware

Section titled “3. BIOS and firmware”

Some systems need proprietary firmware (PSX BIOS, Dreamcast dc_boot.bin, …). GameBEX verifies checksums and places files where emulators read them, and it can fetch missing files from a third-party firmware collection only after you explicitly ask — but fetching does not make it lawful. Dump firmware from hardware you own whenever possible, and treat remote BIOS downloads exactly like ROM downloads under §2.

4. ROM sources (plugins) are third-party files

Section titled “4. ROM sources (plugins) are third-party files”

Download sources are JSON definitions you import; the authors and operators of those sources are third parties this project does not control, endorse, or review. We do not operate any ROM site, do not guarantee any source’s contents or legality, and do not verify that files a source serves match their labels. Importing a source and downloading from it is your decision and your responsibility.

5. Emulators belong to their authors

Section titled “5. Emulators belong to their authors”

All emulation is performed by third-party software — RetroArch and its libretro cores, Dolphin, PPSSPP, melonDS, NetherSX2, and others — each under its own license (many are GPL). GameBEX launches them and, for RetroArch cores, fetches official Libretro buildbot builds; it does not fork, modify, or relicense them. Core/emulator bugs belong upstream; app yours here.

6. Artwork and metadata

Section titled “6. Artwork and metadata”

Cover art and descriptions come from ScreenScraper (which needs your own free account) or from imported sources, or artwork you import yourself. Game art is typically owned by the games’ publishers; it is cached on your device for personal use.

7. Trademarks and affiliation

Section titled “7. Trademarks and affiliation”

PlayStation, Dreamcast, Saturn, Nintendo, SEGA, Game Boy, and all game titles and artwork are trademarks or copyrights of their respective owners. GameBEX is an independent project with no affiliation with, sponsorship by, or endorsement from Sony, Sega, Nintendo, or any emulator author. No OEM names are used to imply endorsement.

8. Warranty and liability

Section titled “8. Warranty and liability”

The software is provided “as is” under the GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later, without warranty of any kind. The authors are not liable for anything you do with it — including what you download, what sources you import, or whether your use complies with your local law.

9. Takedown / contact

Section titled “9. Takedown / contact”

This repository hosts no infringing content, but if you are a rights-holder and believe something here (docs, screenshots, test fixtures) oversteps, open a GitHub issue and it will be addressed promptly.
